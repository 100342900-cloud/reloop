Don't shop for products. Shop for outcomes.

Ask for a working bicycle by Saturday. Independent shops publish their offer policies. Your browser composes a deal that no single site can sell.

How it works

No AI key needed for this demo. Demo clock: Wednesday 6 January 2027 · Saturday is the 9th · budget €100.

  1. Ask for an outcome, not a SKU.
  2. Allow each shop origin, one by one.
  3. Compose a package from published policies, not invented prices.
